federal-contracting-agents is a collection of ready-to-use AI helpers built specifically for people who work with federal contracts. Think of these as smart assistants that can help you with pricing estimates, writing statements of work, and navigating the complex world of government procurement. Whether you're a GS-1102 contracting specialist or run a small business bidding on government work, these tools are designed to make your job easier.
